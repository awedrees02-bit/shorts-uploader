Shorts uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

"Shorts uploader" is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel ClippedByDlow to upload and schedule that channel's own Shorts and to post a creator-credit comment on them. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), it uses the YouTube Data API to: upload videos to the owner's channel, set their privacy and publish time, read the channel name and video titles, and post a comment on the owner's own videos. Scopes: youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl.

What it stores

Only on the owner's own computer: the OAuth login token, a local list of uploaded video IDs and their schedule, and a daily quota counter. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising. No data about other people is collected.

How long it keeps data

Video IDs and the upload list are kept only while needed to manage the channel's uploads. Running the tool's revoke command, or removing access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, ends its access; the local token is then deleted.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: comment on any ClippedByDlow video or message the channel on YouTube.
